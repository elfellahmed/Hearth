CleanHearth is a quiet home screen for rooted LG webOS TVs: your photo, a small clock and your apps, laid out as a Dock (a glass bar along the bottom) or a List (large app names on the left).

  • Opens instead of LG Home: the Home button, Back out of an app and the TV's start.
  • Apps drawn as clean white marks; inputs, LG Home and Settings as quiet corner controls.
  • Wallpapers, slideshow and screen saver; OLED care; no ads.
  • Works next to Glasshouse (keep Glasshouse Home switched off).

Design after CleanFrame by Nathan Clark; built on Hearth by rudra-avatar. MIT licence.
